processes and jobs
OS execute software split into kernels and processes. In linux, the running programs are tracked as processes or background jobs , which can be monitored , controlled , backgrounded,and terminated using shell operations
solve:
ps aux , ps-ef display target process IDs and command parameters 
Job control signals can be suspend processes using cntrl+z and send them background (bg) or bring them foreground (fg). 
SIGTERM can be sent to kill the PIDS
